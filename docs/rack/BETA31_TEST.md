# Beta 31 test in Cubase (about 15 minutes)

Tick each line, or write what happened instead. A screenshot helps whenever something looks wrong.

## Before you start

- Install **Beta 31** (GitHub: Actions > Sonicology suite > the "Beta 31" run > the Windows installer).
  All three plug-ins must be Beta 31: Voxology **0.69.0**, Mixology **0.39.0**, Masterology **0.43.0**.
- Open a song that has:
  - a lead vocal and a backup vocal, each with **Voxology**, then **Mixology Link** after it;
  - a beat or drum track with **Mixology Link**;
  - **Mixology**, then **Masterology**, on the Stereo Out.

## 1. The new buttons (1 minute)

- [ ] Voxology, Masterology and Mixology Link show the word **RACK** (no cube).
- [ ] Mixology: **RACK** is a wide tab after PRESETS, and there's no **?** button.
- [ ] Mixology: **Settings** (the gear) > **Manual** opens the manual.

## 2. The Coach on the backup vocal, after APPLY (4 minutes)

1. Open the Rack > pick the backup vocal > **COACH** > **ANALYZE & BUILD**, and play the song.
   - [ ] While it listens, **STOP** works (it stops listening and nothing changes). Then press ANALYZE & BUILD again.
2. When the plan shows, press **APPLY TO TRACK**.
3. Press **ANALYZE AGAIN** and play again.
   - [ ] It no longer gives the same advice: the modules say **Already set**, and the button says **NOTHING TO CHANGE**
     (or it lists just one or two small changes).
4. - [ ] **UNDO** takes the plan back.

## 3. The Coach on the beat or drum track (3 minutes)

- [ ] **COACH** > **ANALYZE & BUILD** shows a plan. If Mixology hasn't heard the track yet, it listens first: play a part
  where the track plays.
- [ ] **APPLY TO TRACK** changes the sound; **UNDO** takes it back.

## 4. The Coach on the master (4 minutes)

1. Pick **Master** > **COACH**. Choose a GENRE and TARGET > **ANALYZE & BUILD**, and play the song.
   - [ ] After about 12 seconds, **BUILD NOW** appears. Press it.
2. - [ ] **APPLY TO MASTER** changes the sound, and Masterology's own window shows the new settings.
3. - [ ] **ANALYZE AGAIN** (play, then BUILD NOW) says **NOTHING TO CHANGE**.
4. - [ ] **UNDO** takes it back.

## 5. WHAT TO FIX FIRST (3 minutes)

1. In Mixology: **Overview** > **ANALYZE MIX**, and play the busiest part of the song until it finishes.
2. Open the Rack > **SESSION**.
   - [ ] The list on the right shows numbered points, worst first, each naming its tracks.
   - [ ] Clicking a track name opens that track. **COACH** on a point opens the Coach for its track.
   - [ ] The points make sense for your song. Tell me any that don't.

## 6. The whole time

- [ ] No cut-outs or clicks while the Coach or Analyze Mix works.
- [ ] Cubase didn't freeze, and the Rack didn't get slow.
