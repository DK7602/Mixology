# Beta 33 test in Cubase (about 20 minutes)

Tick each line, or write what happened instead. A screenshot helps whenever something looks wrong.

## Before you start

- All three must be Beta 33: Voxology **0.71.0**, Mixology **0.41.0**, Masterology **0.45.0**.
- Open a song with: a lead vocal with **Voxology**, then **Mixology Link**; a beat or drum track with **Mixology Link**;
  **Mixology**, then **Masterology**, on the Stereo Out.

## 1. Move a module (new, 7 minutes)

1. Open the Rack on the lead vocal (RACK page) and play the song.
   - [ ] In SIGNAL CHAIN, Tone EQ, Dynamic EQ, De-Esser, Rider, Compressor and Saturation show six dots on the right;
     Pitch, Cleanup, Delay, Reverb and Output don't.
2. Drag **Compressor** up above **Tone EQ** and let go.
   - [ ] It lands where you dropped it, and CHAIN FLOW (top) shows the new order.
   - [ ] Only a split-second dip in the sound: no click, no cut-out.
   - [ ] A plain click on a row (no drag) still opens that module.
   - [ ] Pitch won't drag.
3. - [ ] The Rack's **UNDO** (curved arrow) moves it back. **REDO** moves it again.
4. - [ ] **RESET ORDER** (under the rows) puts back Voxology's normal order.
5. Move something again, save the project, close it and open it again.
   - [ ] The order you set is still there.
6. On the beat track's Mixology Link: drag **Width** above **EQ**.
   - [ ] It moves, the sound only dips for a moment, and RESET ORDER puts it back.
7. - [ ] Masterology's rows (pick Master) have no dots.
8. Listen: on the vocal, try the **EQ after the Compressor**, and the **De-Esser after the Compressor**. Tell me if you
   hear a difference, and which you like.

## 2. PREVIEW (Beta 32, 6 minutes)

1. Lead vocal > **COACH** > **ANALYZE & BUILD**, play the song. When the plan shows, press **PREVIEW**.
   - [ ] You hear the plan.
   - [ ] Flip **PLAN / YOURS** a few times: about the same loudness (the bottom bar says how much one is turned down).
   - [ ] **BACK TO YOURS**: your sound is back, nothing changed.
   - [ ] **PREVIEW** again, then **KEEP PLAN**: the plan is applied. **UNDO** takes it back.
   - [ ] With PREVIEW on, close the Rack: a few seconds later your own sound is back.
2. - [ ] PREVIEW on the beat track's Coach works too (its loudness match is an estimate: say if one side is clearly louder).
3. - [ ] PREVIEW on the Master's Coach (ANALYZE & BUILD, then BUILD NOW after about 12 seconds) works too.

## 3. From Beta 31 (4 minutes)

- [ ] After APPLY, **ANALYZE AGAIN** on the vocal says **NOTHING TO CHANGE** (or lists only small changes).
- [ ] Mixology **Overview** > **ANALYZE MIX** (play the busiest part), then the Rack's **SESSION** page: WHAT TO FIX FIRST
  lists numbered points, worst first, that make sense for your song.

## 4. The whole time

- [ ] No cut-outs or clicks; Cubase didn't freeze; the Rack didn't get slow.
