The complete vocal, mixing and mastering suite

  Voxology @VOXOLOGY@     vocals: Auto-Edit, tuning (Honey Tune), cleanup, de-essing, compression, space
  Mixology @MIXOLOGY@     the mix: Analyze Mix, Auto Mix, Balance, Polish, Vocal Pocket, every track's processing
  Masterology @MASTEROLOGY@  mastering: Auto-Master, EQ, glue, imaging, loudness, the maximizer

Thanks for trying the beta. Nothing here is finished: tell us what's confusing, what breaks and what you'd change.

NEW IN THIS BETA: UNDO, A / B AND BYPASS IN THE SONICOLOGY RACK
  On the Rack's RACK page, for the track you picked (the buttons next to RACK / MODULES):
  - UNDO / REDO (the curved arrows): your changes in the Rack, one at a time. Cubase's own undo doesn't cover
    plug-in knobs; this does, for what you changed in the Rack.
  - A / B: two versions of this track's settings. Click B (it starts as a copy of A), change things, then flip
    between A and B to compare.
  - BYPASS: this track's Sonicology plug-ins off, to hear before and after. Click again (or leave the Rack) and
    they come back. Tip: switch on Voxology's MATCH for a fair comparison on a vocal.
  All three plug-ins need this version (Voxology 0.59.0, Mixology 0.29.0, Masterology 0.34.0).

ALSO NEW (BETA 19): VOXOLOGY SOUNDS AND TEACHES BETTER
  - LISTEN (Voxology's Cleanup, Dynamic EQ and De-Esser: the LISTEN button next to ON): hear only what that
    module takes out of your vocal, turned up so you can hear it. On the De-Esser you should hear only the "s"
    sounds; if you hear whole words, it's set too strong. Listening only: never in a bounce.
  - Compressor Style: Clean (as before), FET (grabs the front of each word: upfront, great for rap) or Opto
    (smooth and slow to let go: great for sung R&B). Auto-Edit picks one for your style.
  - Air (Saturation): sparkle on top, added only above 8 kHz so the vocal opens up without getting harsh.
    Auto-Edit adds a little for most styles.

ALSO NEW (BETA 18): A QUICKER, SMARTER SONICOLOGY RACK
  - Faster: knobs and meters in the Rack follow your changes straight away, and the plug-ins only send what the
    Rack is showing (lighter on your computer).
  - Bigger: the plug-in window grows while the Rack is open, and goes back to its size when you click BACK.
  - Mouse wheel over a knob: a small nudge (hold Shift for a finer one). Double-click a knob: back to its default.
  - The value under a knob follows while you drag it.
  - One-click fixes: many red warnings in the Rack Coach (two compressors on one vocal, two reverbs, two tuners,
    Mixology's Limiter before Masterology) now have a button that switches the extra module off. UNDO switches it
    back on.
  - A knob jumps back by itself? The Rack tells you: that track's automation is on Read (R lit in Cubase).
  - A plug-in from an older beta in the project? The Rack Coach tells you to install this beta for all three.
  All three plug-ins need this version (Voxology 0.57.0, Mixology 0.28.0, Masterology 0.33.0).

STILL IN THIS BETA: CHANGE SETTINGS FROM THE SONICOLOGY RACK
  The Rack changes settings, not just shows them, in Voxology, Honey Tune, Mixology Link, Mixology and
  Masterology, on any track. The plug-in that owns a setting makes the change, as if you'd done it in its window.
  - Drag a knob up or down to turn it (hold Shift for fine moves).
  - Click a knob with a few options (Mode, Speed, Character...) to step to the next one.
  - Click a module's ON / OFF at the top right to switch it.
  Knobs that only show something don't turn. When a plug-in can't make a change, the Rack says why. While
  Auto-Edit or Auto-Master listens or works, that plug-in is locked for a moment. Honey Tune applies a drag where
  you let go. Open the Rack with the RACK button in any of the three, and press play first.


WHAT THE INSTALLER PUTS WHERE
  Windows   the plug-ins (VST3) in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
            the bridge scripts (they let Mixology move your DAW's faders) in your Documents folder:
              Cubase      Documents\Steinberg\Cubase\MIDI Remote\Driver Scripts\Local\Mixology
              FL Studio   Documents\Image-Line\FL Studio\Settings\Hardware\Mixology Bridge
              Ableton     Documents\Ableton\User Library\Remote Scripts\MixologyBridge
            this Read Me, the manuals and a spare copy of the bridge scripts in C:\Program Files\Sonicology
            (your DAW keeps its scripts somewhere else? copy them from "Bridge scripts" there)
  Mac       the plug-ins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (VST3) and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (AU)
            the bridge scripts:
              Cubase      ~/Documents/Steinberg/Cubase/MIDI Remote/Driver Scripts/Local/Mixology
              FL Studio   ~/Documents/Image-Line/FL Studio/Settings/Hardware/Mixology Bridge
              Ableton     ~/Music/Ableton/User Library/Remote Scripts/MixologyBridge
            this Read Me and the manuals in /Applications/Sonicology
            (a script missing from your folder: copy it from /Library/Application Support/Sonicology)
  All three are listed under "Sonicology" in your DAW's plug-in list. Each plug-in also has its full manual
  inside (the ? button).

FIRST TIME IN YOUR DAW
  1. Rescan plug-ins (Cubase: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager; FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins;
     Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Logic Pro checks Audio Units when it starts; Cakewalk: Edit >
     Preferences > VST Settings, tick "Rescan Failed Plug-Ins", press Scan). In the plug-in list they are under
     Sonicology (sort by manufacturer). Cakewalk still doesn't show them: Utilities > Cakewalk Plug-in Manager,
     and enable them if they are listed as excluded.
  2. Where they go:
       Voxology         on each vocal track (first)
       Mixology Link    on every track you want Mixology to hear (the last plug-in on the track)
       Mixology         on the master / Stereo Out
       Masterology      on the master, after Mixology
  3. In Mixology's Settings (the gear) pick your DAW and the song's style. All three plug-ins share them: pick
     once, the others follow. Every tip is then written in your DAW's own words.
  4. In Mixology press ANALYZE MIX and play the busiest part of the song (20 seconds is enough). Then try AUTO MIX.
     Every change is listed first; nothing happens until you press Apply, and the History (clock button) undoes
     any of it.


MOVING FADERS
  In Cubase, FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro and Cakewalk, Mixology moves the mixer faders itself (volume, pan,
  mute, solo).
  Mixology's Settings (the gear) has a Bridge tab named after your DAW with the steps; in short:

  The two MIDI ports (every DAW)
            Windows: install loopMIDI (free, tobias-erichsen.de) and add two ports named exactly
            "Mixology to Cubase" and "Cubase to Mixology" (the names say Cubase; they work for every DAW).
            Mac: nothing to do, Mixology makes the two ports itself while it runs (from the moment the project
            plays). Don't restart the DAW to find them (they go away with it): open its MIDI settings again.
  Cubase    Usually finds "Mixology Bridge" on its own (Studio > MIDI Remote). In Studio Setup > MIDI Port Setup,
            untick "In 'All MIDI Inputs'" for both Mixology ports.
  FL Studio Options > MIDI settings. Input: pick "Mixology to Cubase", tick Enable, Controller type
            "Mixology Bridge", Port 200. Output: pick "Cubase to Mixology", Port 200 (the same number).
  Ableton   Restart Live after installing. Settings (Live 11: Preferences) > Link, Tempo & MIDI: in a free Control Surface row pick
            "MixologyBridge", Input "Mixology to Cubase", Output "Cubase to Mixology".
  Logic Pro Nothing to install: Mixology acts as a Mackie Control. Press play once; Logic often finds it by
            itself. If not: Logic Pro > Control Surfaces > Setup > New > Install > Mackie Control >
            Add, then Output Port "Cubase to Mixology", Input Port "Mixology to Cubase".
  Cakewalk  Nothing to install: Mixology acts as a Mackie Control. Preferences > MIDI > Devices: tick
            "Mixology to Cubase" (Inputs) and "Cubase to Mixology" (Outputs). MIDI > Control Surfaces > Add
            (the star) > Mackie Control, Input "Mixology to Cubase", Output "Cubase to Mixology", OK.
  Then open Mixology: its Bridge tab turns green ("Connected to ...").

  Other DAWs
            Mixology tells you every fader move (Auto Mix and Balance list them with the exact dB) and you set
            the faders.

  New in this beta: the FL Studio, Ableton, Logic and Cakewalk bridges. If yours doesn't connect, or a fader
  lands on another dB than Mixology says, open Mixology's Bridge tab, press Copy log and send us what it says
  (with a screenshot of the DAW's fader).
