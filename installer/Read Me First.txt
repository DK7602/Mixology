SONICOLOGY  @LABEL@
The complete vocal, mixing and mastering suite

  Voxology @VOXOLOGY@     vocals: Auto-Edit, tuning (Honey Tune), cleanup, de-essing, compression, space
  Mixology @MIXOLOGY@     the mix: Analyze Mix, Auto Mix, Balance, Polish, Vocal Pocket, every track's processing
  Masterology @MASTEROLOGY@  mastering: Auto-Master, EQ, glue, imaging, loudness, the maximizer

Thanks for trying the beta. Nothing here is finished: tell us what's confusing, what breaks and what you'd change.


WHAT THE INSTALLER PUTS WHERE
  Windows   the plug-ins (VST3) in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
            the bridge scripts (they let Mixology move your DAW's faders) in your Documents folder:
              Cubase      Documents\Steinberg\Cubase\MIDI Remote\Driver Scripts\Local\Mixology
              FL Studio   Documents\Image-Line\FL Studio\Settings\Hardware\Mixology Bridge
              Ableton     Documents\Ableton\User Library\Remote Scripts\MixologyBridge
            this Read Me and the manuals in C:\Program Files\Sonicology
  Mac       the plug-ins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 (VST3) and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (AU)
            the bridge scripts:
              Cubase      ~/Documents/Steinberg/Cubase/MIDI Remote/Driver Scripts/Local/Mixology
              FL Studio   ~/Documents/Image-Line/FL Studio/Settings/Hardware/Mixology Bridge
              Ableton     ~/Music/Ableton/User Library/Remote Scripts/MixologyBridge
            this Read Me and the manuals in /Applications/Sonicology
            (a script missing from your folder: copy it from /Library/Application Support/Sonicology)
  All three are listed under "Sonicology" in your DAW's plug-in list. Each plug-in also has its full manual
  inside (the ? button).

FIRST TIME IN YOUR DAW
  1. Rescan plug-ins (Cubase: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager; FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins;
     Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Logic Pro checks Audio Units when it starts).
  2. Where they go:
       Voxology         on each vocal track (first)
       Mixology Link    on every track you want Mixology to hear (the last plug-in on the track)
       Mixology         on the master / Stereo Out
       Masterology      on the master, after Mixology
  3. In Mixology's Settings (the gear) pick your DAW and the song's style. All three plug-ins share them: pick
     once, the others follow. Every tip is then written in your DAW's own words.
  4. In Mixology press ANALYZE MIX and play the busiest part of the song (20 seconds is enough). Then try AUTO MIX.
     Every change is listed first; nothing happens until you press Apply, and the History (clock button) undoes
     any of it.


MOVING FADERS
  In Cubase, FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro and Cakewalk, Mixology moves the mixer faders itself (volume, pan,
  mute, solo).
  Mixology's Settings (the gear) has a Bridge tab named after your DAW with the steps; in short:

  The two MIDI ports (every DAW)
            Windows: install loopMIDI (free, tobias-erichsen.de) and add two ports named exactly
            "Mixology to Cubase" and "Cubase to Mixology" (the names say Cubase; they work for every DAW).
            Mac: nothing to do, Mixology makes the two ports itself once its window has been open. If your DAW
            started before that, restart the DAW (or refresh its MIDI devices) so it sees them.
  Cubase    Usually finds "Mixology Bridge" on its own (Studio > MIDI Remote). In Studio Setup > MIDI Port Setup,
            untick "In 'All MIDI Inputs'" for both Mixology ports.
  FL Studio Options > MIDI settings. Input: pick "Mixology to Cubase", tick Enable, Controller type
            "Mixology Bridge", Port 200. Output: pick "Cubase to Mixology", Port 200 (the same number).
  Ableton   Restart Live after installing. Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI: in a free Control Surface row pick
            "MixologyBridge", Input "Mixology to Cubase", Output "Cubase to Mixology".
  Logic Pro Nothing to install: Mixology acts as a Mackie Control. Open Mixology's window once; Logic often
            finds it by itself. If not: Logic Pro > Control Surfaces > Setup > New > Install > Mackie Control >
            Add, then Output Port "Cubase to Mixology", Input Port "Mixology to Cubase".
  Cakewalk  Nothing to install: Mixology acts as a Mackie Control. Preferences > MIDI > Devices: tick
            "Mixology to Cubase" (Inputs) and "Cubase to Mixology" (Outputs). MIDI > Control Surfaces > Add
            (the star) > Mackie Control, Input "Mixology to Cubase", Output "Cubase to Mixology", OK.
  Then open Mixology: its Bridge tab turns green ("Connected to ...").

  Other DAWs
            Mixology tells you every fader move (Auto Mix and Balance list them with the exact dB) and you set
            the faders.

  New in this beta: the FL Studio, Ableton, Logic and Cakewalk bridges. If yours doesn't connect, or a fader
  lands on another dB than Mixology says, open Mixology's Bridge tab, press Copy log and send us what it says
  (with a screenshot of the DAW's fader).
