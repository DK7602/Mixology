Sonicology @LABEL@

The complete vocal, mixing and mastering suite: Voxology for vocals, Mixology for the mix, Masterology for mastering. They work together: pick your DAW and the song's style in one, the others follow.

This installs the plug-ins as VST3 and Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins, the bridge scripts that let Mixology move the faders in Cubase, FL Studio and Ableton Live, and the Read Me and manuals in /Applications/Sonicology. Logic Pro needs nothing extra: Mixology works as its Mackie Control (the Read Me says how).

After installing, rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic Pro checks Audio Units when it starts). They are listed under Sonicology.

This is a beta for friends trying it out: thanks! The next page has the Read Me with what to send us.
